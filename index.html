Citizen Rights & Legal Redressal

Interactive Citizen Legal Remedies Roadmap

Know your statutory rights and procedural next steps for common civil, consumer, and criminal issues

🚨 Police Refuses FIR

Statutory Steps under BNSS 2023:

  • Step 1: Send written substance of complaint to Superintendent of Police via registered post under Section 173(4) BNSS.
  • Step 2: If no investigation, file an application before Judicial Magistrate under Section 175(3) BNSS.
💳 Cheque Dishonour

Negotiable Instruments Act (Sec 138):

  • Step 1: Issue statutory legal demand notice within 30 days of receiving memo of dishonour from the bank.
  • Step 2: Give 15 days to payer to make payment.
  • Step 3: File criminal complaint before Magistrate within 1 month of expiry of 15-day period.
🛒 Defective Product / Service

Consumer Protection Act 2019:

  • Step 1: Send formal notice to seller / e-commerce platform giving 15 days to refund/replace.
  • Step 2: File e-complaint on edaakhil.nic.in (no lawyer needed for claims up to Rs 50 Lakhs).
  • Limitation: Within 2 years from cause of action (Section 69).
